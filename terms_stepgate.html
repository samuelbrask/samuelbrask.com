1. The Service

StepGate is a health and productivity app that helps users manage their screen time by requiring physical activity (walking) to earn access to selected apps on their iPhone. The app reads step data from Apple HealthKit and uses Apple's Screen Time API to manage app restrictions.

StepGate is designed for adult self-regulation. Users voluntarily choose to restrict their own app usage as motivation to walk more. It is not a parental control tool.
